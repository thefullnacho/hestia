Expect is what a reliable device must do. `ask` = no call, a clarifying question. `none` = no call. Free-text args pass when every expected word appears in the model's value; enums and numbers must match; unlisted args aren't checked; an arg expected as null must be left out (no room named = the device's room, filled in by code).
